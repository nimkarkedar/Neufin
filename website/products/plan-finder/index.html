What is an energy plan?

Our energy plans give businesses a simple, low-cost way to buy power. Choose your tenure, tariff and capacity to get started, and our experts guide you through the rest. Power can start in as little as 30 days. It's as simple as choosing a mobile phone plan.

The price depends on your state, your site and the term you choose. Your quote gives the exact figure.

Are these prices final?

No. They are indicative; your quote has the final price.

Yes. One contract can cover sites across states. We supply in 16 states.
