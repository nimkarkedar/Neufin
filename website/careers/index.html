Why we started Neufin

Our mission is to turn energy into a competitive advantage for every business.

India is in the middle of the largest energy build-out in human history: 225 GW of new power capacity between 2027 and 2029, on a grid that is being upgraded and expanded in real time. And there is no modern software to run any of it.

We started Neufin Energy because we believe the next decade of Indian energy will be won by teams who can move at software speed across markets, regulation and customer support.

If you want to build that, at the speed it deserves, we’d love to meet you.

Rushil and RahoolCo-founders, Neufin Energy

What it’s actually like to work here

  • Work with people who care

    Sharp, curious people who share what they know, so you learn fast.

  • Own something meaningful

    We are small, so your work changes what businesses pay for power.

  • Move like energy in the grid

    Power markets move by the hour, so we decide fast and ship often.

How we work: our values

  • Extreme ownership

    Own the outcome, with no excuses or blame.

  • Customer obsession

    Start with the customer, and make sure our work adds value.

  • Solution mindset

    Solve or work around problems. Don't just report them.

  • Show respect

    Be honest, challenge ideas directly, and commit once we decide.
